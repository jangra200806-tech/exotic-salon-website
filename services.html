Salon services in Kaithal — common questions

Price, timing aur booking se judi baatein, simple answers mein.

Kaithal mein haircut ka price kitna hai?

Men's haircut ₹200 se aur women's haircut ₹350 se shuru hota hai. Final price hair length, style aur chosen service par depend karta hai — booking se pehle team confirm kar deti hai.

Bridal makeup ka package kitne ka hai?

Bridal makeup ₹8,000 se shuru hota hai (approx. 3 hours). Isme HD makeup, hairdo aur draping included hai. Trial, jewellery setting ya home service add karni ho to WhatsApp par custom quote le sakte hain.

Keratin aur hair smoothening mein kya difference hai?

Keratin hair ko protein treatment se smooth aur shiny banata hai, jabki smoothening frizz control ke liye hair bonds ko relax karti hai. Dono ki duration aur aftercare alag hoti hai — stylist aapke hair type ke hisaab se sahi option suggest karega.

Kya bina appointment aa sakte hain?

Haan, walk-ins welcome hain. Weekend aur shaadi season mein wait se bachne ke liye pehle call ya WhatsApp par slot confirm kar lena better hai.
